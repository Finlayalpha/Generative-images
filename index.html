      How results are chosen

      A place is listed only if it is free and legal for your whole stay and your vehicle fits. Where a street's parking rules are mapped in OpenStreetMap we use them. Everywhere else we apply UK national rules (Highway Code): no parking within 10 m of a junction, on crossing zig-zags, at bus stops, across driveways and dropped kerbs, in cycle and bus lanes, or by double white lines. Those streets are marked Unsurveyed: always check for yellow lines and signs.

      Where we have aerial photos, kerbs with yellow lines, zig-zags, red routes or bus lanes are removed, and kerbs with no lines are marked Lines checked with the photo date. Photos can't show the times on signs, so marked bays are left out unless their rules are known.

      Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. A guide only: the signs and lines on the street always take priority.
