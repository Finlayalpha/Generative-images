      How results are ranked

      Only places that are free and legal for your whole stay are listed. Likelihood = chance the mapped rules are right × chance at least one space is empty. Space counts are estimated from kerb length when not mapped, and occupancy is an assumption until real-time data is added. Best match balances both: a spot 400 m further away needs to be about 2.7× as likely to rank the same.

      Regulations from OpenStreetMap contributors. Always check the signs on the street: data may be missing or out of date.
